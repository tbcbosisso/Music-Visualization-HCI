The arrangement

How raw rows became this score.

Six steps in build_music_csv.py.

  1. LoadRead music.xlsx, sheet “music”.
  2. CleanDrop year 0 and negative hotness placeholders.
  3. WindowKeep 1960 to 2010.
  4. MapAbout 80 messy genre tags become a few clean families.
  5. AggregateOne row per genre-year: artists, hotness, songs.
  6. FilterDrop genre-years with fewer than 3 artists.

Blank cells mean “too thin to trust”, not zero. This is a sample of the industry, so read patterns, not exact market size.
